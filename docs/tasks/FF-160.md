# FF-160 A tidier rolls page header

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 3. Rolls and the roll page

## Why

The controls above the rolls had grown to six, running on to two lines. Rarely used choices and bulk actions shouldn't take as much room as the ones used all the time.

## What

On one row: the layout as two icons, a "View options" menu holding Group by and Sort by, and "New roll" with a small arrow joined to its right, whose menu holds "Bulk import rolls…" and "Re-number rolls…". While previewing numbers, what will change and Apply and Cancel are in a bar above the rolls, so the header never grows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The rolls page's controls fit on one row at 1280px: the layout icons, "View options" and "New roll" with its arrow.
- [x] Grid and List are two icon buttons named "Grid" and "List" (also their hover text), the one in use pressed.
- [x] "View options" opens a panel with "Group by" and "Sort by", as before, kept open while they're changed and closed by Esc or a click elsewhere; the address keeps both.
- [x] "New roll" is still the primary button to the new roll page; its arrow, named "More roll actions", opens a menu of "Bulk import rolls…" (to the import page) and "Re-number rolls…" (which starts the preview).
- [x] While numbers are previewed, a bar above the rolls says "Preparing preview…", then how many numbers will change ("3 numbers will change") with "Apply" and "Cancel", or "The rolls are in order." with "Cancel".
- [x] Nothing else about numbering changes: the preview on the rolls, the messages, and what Apply does.

## Not in this task

- The Events page's own "Group by" and the roll page's controls.
- Putting the Browse panel's filters in the menu.

## Notes

- The View options panel is its own small component (`rolls/view-options.tsx`), not a menu of actions, because it holds two selects; the layout icons are `rolls/view-switch.tsx`; the arrow is the existing `MenuButton`.
- Tests: rolls page tests for the panel (closed, open, kept open, Esc), the icons, the menu, and the numbering bar; the journeys that grouped, numbered or imported now go through the panel and the menu.

## What was built

`rolls/rolls-page.tsx` replaces its row of buttons with `ViewSwitch`, `ViewOptions` and a `split-button` (a `New roll` link and a `MenuButton` with a chevron, joined), and shows the numbering preview's progress, count, Apply and Cancel in a `numbering-bar` above the rolls. Styles are at the end of `styles.css`.
