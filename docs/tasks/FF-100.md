# FF-100 Stars on thumbnails

**Status:** dropped · **Was:** U31

*Split into [FF-148 Stars on thumbnails](FF-148.md), showing the stars and marks, and [FF-149 Rate from a thumbnail](FF-149.md), rating by clicking them. The criteria below are as they were when it was split.*

## Why

So the frames worth a look stand out without opening each, a frame can be rated straight from a contact sheet, and a frame says the same about itself wherever it's shown.

## What

Every frame thumbnail shows the frame's rating as small filled stars at its top left; pointing at the thumbnail shows the other, outlined stars too, and clicking one rates the frame (D62). The marks U69 and U70 added to the roll page (its own details or notes, and more than one version) come to the event page and the filmstrip, so the roll page, the event page and the frame view's filmstrip show the same marks.

## Acceptance criteria

Showing:

- [ ] On a roll's page, an event's page and the frame view's filmstrip, a frame rated 1 to 5 shows that many filled stars at its thumbnail's top left (★★★★ for 4), and a frame with no rating shows none.
- [ ] The stars sit straight on the picture, in the accent colour, with a soft shadow so they read over a bright scan; no badge behind them. In the filmstrip they're smaller, in the same corner.
- [ ] Pointing at a thumbnail shows all five stars, those beyond its rating outlined (★★★★☆), and an unrated frame shows five outlined stars (☆☆☆☆☆).
- [ ] Pointing at a star fills it and the stars before it in a dimmer amber, as a preview of the rating a click would give, with a pointer cursor, so it's clear a click rates the frame rather than opening it.
- [ ] The stars are named "Rated 4 of 5" for screen readers and on hover, and "Not rated" for a frame with no rating; each star is named "Rate 3 stars", and the current rating's star "Clear rating".

Rating:

- [ ] Clicking a star rates the frame with that many stars, without opening it, on the roll page, the event page and in the filmstrip, saved with `If-Match`.
- [ ] Clicking the star of the rating it already has clears it, as in the frame view (D39).
- [ ] The new rating shows at once on that thumbnail, and on the others showing that frame (the filmstrip and the frame view's heading) straight away.
- [ ] A save refused because the roll changed (409) puts the stars back as they were and says so, as rating in the frame view does.
- [ ] On touch screens, with no pointing, thumbnails show the filled stars only and a tap opens the frame as now; rating is done in the frame view.
- [ ] The thumbnail stars aren't in the Tab order: from the keyboard, frames are rated in the frame view (its stars, or the 1 to 5 keys), as now.

Other states and marks:

- [ ] While bulk editing on the roll page, the stars are hidden and the checkbox takes the corner; they come back on Done.
- [ ] While renumbering, the stars show but can't be clicked.
- [ ] The event page and the filmstrip also show U69's icon (details of its own or notes) and U70's (more than one version) at the bottom left, named as on the roll page.

## Not in this task

- Rating several frames at once (from bulk edit's Set menu).
- Rating from the keyboard or by touch on thumbnails.
- Showing U69 and U70's icons anywhere else (rolls page strips, events page cards).

## Notes

- Ratings are one to five stars, or none (D39); clearing is by clicking the current rating, as in the frame view (D62).
- The stars and icons move into one shared piece drawn with `Thumbnail` (from the roll page's `FrameMarks`), so the three places can't drift apart. The stars sit outside the thumbnail's link, or stop the click reaching it, so rating never opens the frame.
- Saving uses the frame view's queued saves (`useSaveFrame`) on the frame view's filmstrip, and the roll's save with `If-Match` on the roll and event pages.
- The filmstrip's thumbnails are decorative, named by their links, so the link's name also says the rating ("Frame 12, rated 4 of 5").
- The frame view's heading keeps its filled stars (★★★★) beside the frame number.
- Tests: roll page, event page and frame view tests for showing (rated, unrated, pointing), rating and clearing by click, a 409, bulk editing and renumbering, and the icons on the event page and filmstrip. No journey.
