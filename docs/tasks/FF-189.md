# FF-189 An event's page in a site: a big title and a grid that fills each row

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

Seeing an event's photos together, large and in their own shapes, so the page looks considered rather than like a contact sheet of squares.

## What

An event's page opens with its name in very large type and its date and photo count beside it, over a thin black rule. Its photos follow in rows that line up at both edges, each photo keeping its shape, so upright and sideways frames sit together. At the foot, links go to the next newer and older events. A roll's page uses the same layout. _(From the [wireframes](https://claude.ai/artifact/VWNcdhqokFEmgHq6My8bqn): "Event" and "Phone · Event". Replaces the event and roll pages of FF-183.)_

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The header is the site's (FF-188), with "All events" at the right linking home.
- [ ] The title block has the event's name in very large bold type and, beside it on a wide screen and under it on a phone, "Sep 2026 · 42 photos"; a thin black rule under it.
- [ ] The photos are in shot order, in rows that fill the width with even gaps, every photo in a row the same height and none cropped; the last row isn't stretched beyond a normal row's height.
- [ ] Each photo links to its frame page (FF-190) and has its `srcset` from the site's sizes.
- [ ] At the foot, "Newer" and "Earlier" links show the neighbouring events' names ("Earlier · Lake District →"); the newest has no Newer, the oldest no Earlier.
- [ ] A roll's page has the same layout, titled "2026-053 Westfjords", with no Newer or Earlier links.
- [ ] On a phone, rows hold fewer photos (one or two) so each stays a useful size; nothing scrolls sideways.
- [ ] No inline styles: each photo's share of its row comes from classes in the stylesheet, as the photo shapes do now; the page works without a script.

## Not in this task

- An event's description: events have none yet.
- Other layouts (one photo at a time down the page, as in the wireframes' "Sequence").

## Notes

- The rows are worked out by FilmFile from each photo's width and height (a target row shape, as justified galleries do), not in the browser, so the page needs no script and no inline styles; the phone layout comes from the stylesheet's media query.
- Needs FF-188 (the header and the look). D107.
