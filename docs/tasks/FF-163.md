# FF-163 Add selected frames to a site or export

**Status:** later · **Group:** 13. Sites

## What

While selecting frames on a roll's page or an event's page, the Set menu offers "Add to site…", "Leave off site…", "Add to export…" and "Leave off export…": choose a site or export and the frames are pinned or hidden on it (their "Always include" and "Never include"), without opening its page.

## Acceptance criteria

Written when the task is planned into a group.
