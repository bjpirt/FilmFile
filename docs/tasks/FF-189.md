# FF-189 An event's page in a site: a big title and a grid that fills each row

**Status:** done · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Built in:** 13. Sites

## Why

Seeing an event's photos together, large and in their own shapes, so the page looks considered rather than like a contact sheet of squares.

## What

An event's page opens with its name in very large type and its date and photo count beside it, over a thin black rule. Its photos follow in rows that line up at both edges, each photo keeping its shape, so upright and sideways frames sit together. At the foot, links go to the next newer and older events. A roll's page uses the same layout. _(From the [wireframes](https://claude.ai/artifact/VWNcdhqokFEmgHq6My8bqn): "Event" and "Phone · Event". Replaces the event and roll pages of FF-183.)_

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The header is the site's (FF-188), with "All events" at the right linking home (on frame pages too); the "← All events" link under it is gone.
- [x] The title block has the event's name in very large bold type and, beside it on a wide screen and under it on a phone, "Sep 2026 · 42 photos" (an event with no date of its own takes its first frame's, as its card does; a year's other photos show just their count); a thin black rule under it.
- [x] The photos are in shot order, in rows that fill the width with even gaps, every photo in a row the same height and none cropped; the last row isn't stretched beyond a normal row's height.
- [x] Each photo links to its frame page (FF-190) and has its `srcset` from the site's sizes.
- [x] At the foot, "Newer" and "Earlier" links show the neighbouring cards' names in the home page's order ("Newer ← Shetland coast", "Earlier Other 2019 photos →"), a year's other photos included; the newest has no Newer, the oldest no Earlier.
- [x] A site names no roll anywhere (D107): the "By roll" list, the roll pages and their frame pages are gone, the service serves nothing under `rolls/`, and a photo's alt text is its title, else "Photo from Iceland 2026" (its event), "Photo from 12 Sep 2026" (its date) or "Photo", never "Roll 2026-053, frame 2". _(Changed after review, from roll pages in the same layout.)_
- [x] On a phone, rows hold fewer photos (one or two) so each stays a useful size; nothing scrolls sideways.
- [x] No inline styles: each photo's share of its row comes from classes in the stylesheet, as the photo shapes do now; the page works without a script.

## What was built

- Domain (`domain/sites/`): `siteGroups` makes only events, `renderSite` no roll pages and `renderHomePage` no "By roll" list; `BuildSite` writes the alt text from the title, event or date; `GetBuiltSiteFile` serves only `events/` pages. `renderGroupPage` has the title block (`.title-block`: the name as `h1`, `.group-meta` beside it) and, for an event, `.neighbours` ("Other events") with its newer and earlier cards; `eventSections` (moved out of the home page) gives the order both use. `allEventsLink` puts "All events" in the header of every page but the home page.
- The stylesheet has the title block (stacked on a phone), the header link and the Newer and Earlier links.
- Tests: the event page (header, title block, its date or its first frame's, neighbours in the home page's order and their ends, no back link), no roll anywhere, the alt text, and `build-site.e2e.ts` (no roll named, the event's neighbour, home by "All events").

## Not in this task

- An event's description: events have none yet.
- Other layouts (one photo at a time down the page, as in the wireframes' "Sequence").
