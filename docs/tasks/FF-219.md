# FF-219 Resolve a frame's details in one place

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 16. Internal improvements

## Why

How a frame's details are layered (the archive's settings, then its roll's, then its own: design section 6) is a rule, and it's written out in four use cases. A change to the layering would have to be made four times.

## What

One domain function, `resolveFrameDetails(settings, roll, frame)`, gives a frame's details, and the four use cases call it. Nothing changes in behaviour. _(Recommendation R9a of the architecture review.)_

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `apps/backend/src/domain/details/resolve-frame-details.ts` exports `resolveFrameDetails(settings: Settings | undefined, roll: Roll, frame: Frame)`, giving what `resolveDetails([archiveLayer(settings), rollLayer(roll), frameLayer(frame)])` gives now, with the archive layer left out when there are no settings. Its TSDoc cites design section 6.
- [ ] Unit tests in `resolve-frame-details.test.ts`: a detail from each layer, a frame's own overriding its roll's and the archive's, and no settings.
- [ ] `get-frame-details.ts`, `search-frames.ts`, `make-quick-export.ts` and `build-site.ts` call it, and none of them builds the layers itself.
- [ ] All tests pass unchanged.
