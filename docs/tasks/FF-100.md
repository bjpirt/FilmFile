# FF-100 Stars on thumbnails

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 1. Multi-version frames, finished off · **Was:** U31

## Why

So the frames worth a look stand out without opening each.

## What

Wherever a frame's thumbnail is shown (a roll's page, an event's page and the frame view's filmstrip), it shows the frame's rating as small stars (D39). Its notes icon became U69.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A frame rated 1 to 5 shows that many small filled stars on its thumbnail on a roll's page, on an event's page and in the frame view's filmstrip.
- [ ] The stars are named "Rated 4 of 5" for screen readers and on hover.
- [ ] A frame with no rating shows no stars.
- [ ] Rating a frame in the frame view updates its stars in the filmstrip straight away, and on the roll page when it's next shown.
- [ ] The stars sit where they don't cover U69 and U70's icons, the checkbox or the delete icon.

## Not in this task

- Rating from the roll page.

## Notes

- Ratings are one to five stars, or none (D39).
- Tests: roll page, event page and filmstrip tests for a rated and an unrated frame.
