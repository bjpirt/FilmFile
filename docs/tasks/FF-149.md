# FF-149 Rate from a thumbnail

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 5. Describing frames

## Why

Rating a roll's frames straight from its contact sheet, without opening each.

## What

Pointing at a thumbnail on the roll page or the event page shows all five of its stars (FF-148), the rest outlined, in the place the plain stars are, and clicking one rates the frame rather than opening it (D62). The rating shows at once and is saved in the background. *(Split from FF-100.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Pointing at a thumbnail on the roll page or the event page shows all five stars at its top left, straight on the picture with no box behind them and in the same place and size as the plain stars, so the filled ones don't move, those beyond its rating outlined (★★★★☆); an unrated frame shows five outlined stars (☆☆☆☆☆). Without pointing, a thumbnail looks as in FF-148 (filled stars only, nothing for an unrated frame).
- [x] Pointing at a star fills it and the stars before it in a dimmer amber, as a preview of the rating a click would give, with a pointer cursor, so it's clear a click rates the frame rather than opening it.
- [x] Each star is a button named "Rate 3 stars" (also its hover text), and the star of the frame's current rating is named "Clear rating"; the five stars of an unrated frame are grouped as "Not rated". The buttons are skipped by Tab (`tabindex="-1"`) but reachable by a screen reader.
- [x] Clicking a star rates the frame with that many stars, without opening it, on the roll page and on the event page, saved with `If-Match` on the frame's own roll.
- [x] Clicking the star of the rating the frame already has clears it, as in the frame view (D39).
- [x] The new rating shows at once on that thumbnail and is saved in the background; saves for a roll run one after another against its latest version, so quick clicks on several frames never clash. It shows straight away wherever else that frame is showing (the filmstrip and the frame view's heading).
- [x] A save refused because the roll changed (409) puts the stars back as they were and shows "This roll changed since you opened it. The rating wasn't saved. Reload the page." at the top of the page, and the roll is read again so the next click works. Any other failed save puts the stars back and shows "Couldn't save the rating. Is the service running?".
- [x] On touch screens, with no pointing, thumbnails show the filled stars only and a tap opens the frame as now; rating is done in the frame view.
- [x] From the keyboard, frames are rated in the frame view (its stars, or the 1 to 5 keys), as now.
- [x] While renumbering, the stars show but can't be clicked; while bulk editing, they're hidden (FF-148).
- [x] The filmstrip's thumbnails show their stars as in FF-148 and can't be clicked to rate; the frame view's own stars and 1 to 5 keys rate there.

## What was built

`rolls/use-rate-frame.ts` (`useRateFrame`) rates a frame from its thumbnail: the new rating is shown at once in the roll's cached data (`['rolls', rollId]`) and the rolls list (`['rolls']`), so the filmstrip, the frame view's heading and the event page follow, and is saved in the background with `PATCH /api/rolls/{rollId}/frames/{frameId}` and `If-Match`. Each roll's saves run one after another, taking the roll's version from the cache (or reading the roll, on the event page) and keeping only the new version after a save. A 409 or any other failure puts the rating back and gives the page's message; a 409 also reads the rolls again. `RatingStrip` in `rolls/frame-marks.tsx` draws the five buttons ("Rate 3 stars", "Clear rating", grouped "Not rated" or "Rated 2 of 5", `tabindex="-1"`, previewing the pointed-at rating) when `FrameMarks` is given `rate`, which the roll page passes unless renumbering or selecting and the event page always passes; the filmstrip doesn't. The stars are drawn by CSS (`::before`), so they add no text to a frame's caption, and shown, in the plain stars' place with the same shadow and no box, only while the pointer is over the thumbnail (`@media (hover: hover)`), so touch screens are unchanged.

Tests: roll page (names and Tab order, preview, rating and clearing, no navigation, quick clicks on two frames, a 409, a failed save, none while renumbering or selecting), event page (rated against its own roll, a failed save) and frame view (no rating buttons in the filmstrip). Hover and touch are CSS, so they're checked by eye, not by a test. No journey, as planned.

## Not in this task

- Rating from the filmstrip's thumbnails: they're small, and the frame being viewed has its stars and keys. (D62 now says so.)
- Rating several frames at once (from bulk edit's Set menu).
- Rating by touch on thumbnails, or by keyboard on thumbnails.

## Notes

- Needs FF-148.
- Decisions D62 (rating on thumbnails, now roll and event pages only, with no backing), D39 (one to five stars, none cleared by clicking the current one).
- The stars are drawn by `FrameMarks` (`rolls/frame-marks.tsx`); they gain the five-star strip and buttons on the roll page and event page only (an option on `FrameMarks`), outside the thumbnail's link, or stopping the click reaching it, so rating never opens the frame. Hover only (`@media (hover: hover)`).
- Saving: optimistic update of the roll's cached data, then a queue of `PATCH /api/rolls/{rollId}/frames/{frameId}` with `rating` and `If-Match`, per roll; the event page, whose frames come from several rolls, reads and saves each frame's own roll. A failure restores the previous rating and the roll's data is read again. Reuses the frame view's save queue where it fits (`useSaveFrame`), without its single-frame assumption.
- The top-of-page message is the page's existing problem line.
- Tests: roll page and event page tests for pointing, the preview, rating and clearing by click, the queue, a 409 and another failure (stars put back, message), touch (no pointing), keyboard (not in Tab order), renumbering and bulk editing; frame view tests that the filmstrip's stars aren't clickable and update when a frame is rated elsewhere. No journey.
