# FF-188 A site's home page: its events by year

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

A public site is about what happened, not which roll it was on, so a visitor should start from the events, newest first, in a look that's graphic and minimal and leaves the photos to carry the colour.

## What

A built site's `index.html` becomes its events grouped under large year headings, each event a card with one upright cover photo, its name, month and photo count. There's no Rolls view: the rolls are a short "By roll" list in small type at the foot of the page. "Organise by" goes from the site's page. The whole site takes the wireframes' look: white, black type, thin black rules, large bold headings, no colour but the photos. _(From the [wireframes](https://claude.ai/artifact/VWNcdhqokFEmgHq6My8bqn): "Home" and "Phone · Home". Replaces the cards of FF-183.)_

## Acceptance criteria

The page:

- [ ] `index.html` has the site's name at the top left, linking home, and "6 events · 139 photographs" at the right.
- [ ] Under it, a section for each year that has events, newest first, headed by the year in large type over a thin black rule.
- [ ] Each year holds a card for each of its events, newest first: one cover photo, cropped upright (4:5), then the event's name and "Sep · 42" (its month and photo count); the card links to the event's page.
- [ ] An event's cover is its highest-rated frame on the site, the first in shot order when ratings tie.
- [ ] An event's year and month are its date's; an event with no date takes its first frame's date shot.
- [ ] Frames in no event are a "No event" card after the last year, as an event would be. _(To settle when refined.)_
- [ ] At the foot, "By roll" and a list in small grey type of the site's rolls ("2026-053 Westfjords"), newest first, each linking to its roll's page.
- [ ] On a phone the cards are two to a row and the year headings smaller; nothing scrolls sideways.

The look (every page):

- [ ] White, near-black text (#111) and grey (#6b6b6b, 4.5:1 or better) for secondary text, thin rules, large bold headings with tight spacing, and no colour but the photos; no shadows, rounded corners or gradients.
- [ ] Pages still use no inline styles and work without a script (D102).

On the site's page in the app:

- [ ] "Organise by" is gone; a site is always organised by event. A site saved with `organiseBy` still reads, and the field is dropped the next time it's saved.

Files:

- [ ] `rolls.html` and the Events | Rolls menu are gone; a roll's page (`rolls/<roll page name>.html`) stays, reached from the "By roll" list, and its frame pages with it.

## Not in this task

- An event's page (FF-189) and a frame's page (FF-190).
- Choosing an event's cover by hand, or an event's description.
- Paging or "load more" for a site with many events.
