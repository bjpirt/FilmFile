# FF-104 Mark frames to scan

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 1. Multi-version frames, finished off · **Was:** U22a

## Why

While looking through a roll's lab scans, I can set aside the frames worth scanning myself, and see which they are.

## What

A mark on a frame saying it's to be scanned at higher resolution, set one at a time or in bulk, shown on its thumbnail, and gone once it has a Raw scan. *(Split from U22.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In the frame view, "Mark to scan" in the bar marks the frame; it then reads "Marked to scan", and choosing it again takes the mark off.
- [ ] While bulk editing, Set ▸ "Mark to scan" and Set ▸ "Unmark" mark or unmark every selected frame in one save.
- [ ] A marked frame shows a scanner icon in its thumbnail's row of icons (U69, U70), named "To scan at higher resolution".
- [ ] The roll page's heading says how many: "4 frames to scan", and nothing when none are.
- [ ] Adding a Raw scan to a marked frame takes the mark off.
- [ ] A save refused because the roll changed (409) leaves the mark as it was and says so.

## Not in this task

- The scan list, and matching raws to it (FF-131).

## Notes

- roll.json: the frame's `toScan: true`, only when set.
- API: `PATCH …/frames/{frameId}` and `PATCH …/frames` take `toScan`.
