# U10a Record a roll's film stock

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 4. Metadata

## What was built

Film stocks as equipment records (`equipment/films/<slug>.json`: make, name, ISO, type and format; D29), listed and added through `GET`/`POST /api/equipment/films`. The new roll page and Edit details have a Film stock dropdown of the archive's film stocks, whose last option, Add new…, asks for the new film stock's details. A roll keeps its film stock's slug in `meta.film`. Shown in the details row, on cards ("Nikon FE · Kodak Portra 400 · Sep 2026") and as a List column.
