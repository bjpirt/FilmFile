# FF-234 The site page's Publish section

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

Publishing a site, and seeing whether what's on the web matches it, from the site's own page. _(Split from FF-201, which needs to be built first.)_

## What

A "Publish" section on the site's page, below Build, says where the site goes (a Cloudflare account ID, a bucket and the address it's seen at, the bucket used only for this site) and whether what's there matches the site. "Publish" builds as needed and uploads, with progress and "Cancel". "Unpublish" removes it, after asking.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The site's page has a "Publish" section below Build, with fields "Cloudflare account ID", "Bucket" and "Address", saved with the site as they're changed, like its other details. The Bucket field's hint reads "Use a bucket only for this site: files it doesn't have are removed." Until all three are filled in, the section reads "Say where to publish the site." and "Publish" is disabled.
- [ ] A field that isn't valid says why under it and isn't saved: an account ID that isn't 32 hex characters, a bucket name R2 wouldn't take, an address that isn't an `https://` URL. The address is kept and opened as typed.
- [ ] When the page opens, with a target set, the section says how the bucket stands: "Not published", "Published 13 Nov 2026, 14:02, up to date" or "Published 13 Nov 2026, 14:02: 12 files to update". While it's asking it reads "Checking what's published…". After a build, publish or unpublish it asks again.
- [ ] When the check fails, the failure's message shows where the status would be, with a "Check again" button; "Publish" stays enabled. Nothing is retried on its own.
- [ ] "Publish" builds the site first when it isn't built or is out of date ("Making images: 12 of 40"), then uploads ("Uploading 12 of 40"), with "Cancel" throughout. When it's done it reads "Published 13 Nov 2026, 14:02, up to date", with "View site" opening the address. A cancelled publish reads "Publishing cancelled."
- [ ] "Unpublish" asks "Unpublish Iceland? This removes 2,104 files from photos-pirt.", with "Unpublish" and "Cancel", then reads "Not published". With nothing published it's disabled.
- [ ] Build, Publish and Unpublish are disabled while any of them runs for the site, and while a change to the site is being saved.
- [ ] A failed publish or unpublish shows the failure's message from the service (FF-201), and says anything already uploaded stays.
- [ ] The section can be used with the keyboard and its fields, buttons and progress have accessible names; on a phone it stacks under Build without scrolling sideways.

## Not in this task

- The service's work: the routes, uploading and the keys (FF-201).
- A folder in the bucket (FF-235), S3 (FF-200) and publishing automatically on a change.

## Notes

- Uses the routes of FF-201 (built). When the bucket can't be asked, `GET /api/sites/{slug}/published` answers `stands: "unknown"` with the failure's message in `check`, which is what the section shows beside "Check again".
- Uses the routes of FF-201; the build's progress display (`build-panel.tsx`) is the model for the section's.
- **Tests:** web (the section's states and messages from the fake archive), and a journey that sets a target, publishes to the in-memory bucket, sees "up to date", changes a frame, sees "files to update", and unpublishes.
