# FF-120 Make an event's site

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 7. Event sites · **Was:** U98

## Why

Sharing an event as a simple website that works from any host, or none.

## What

"Make site" on an event's page writes a self-contained static site of its chosen frames (FF-119) into `Sites/<event>/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] "Make site" writes `Sites/iceland-2026/` with an `index.html` showing the event's name, dates and notes, and its chosen frames in shot order.
- [ ] Each frame opens larger, with its title and, if chosen, its camera, lens and film stock.
- [ ] Images are web sized, in a few widths for different screens; originals are never copied.
- [ ] No GPS or other location is in the images or pages, and the images' metadata is only the author and copyright.
- [ ] The site works opened straight from the folder, needs nothing from the internet, and works on a phone.
- [ ] Making it again replaces the folder with the current frames.
- [ ] Then: "Site made in Sites/iceland-2026: 24 photos."

## Not in this task

- Publishing it (FF-121).
- Sites of more than one event, or of hand-picked albums.

## Notes

- The pages are made by pure functions (frames and settings in, files out), tested without a browser.
- Journey: make a site and open its index in the browser.
