# U52 Film stock is the roll's

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 3. Rolls and the roll page

## What was built

A frame can no longer have its own film stock (D45): it's gone from "Set film stock…" while selecting, from a frame's details in the frame view (always the roll's, so no "Use the roll's" or "Set on roll instead" for it), from the "Own" mark, and from `PATCH …/frames/{frameId}`, `PATCH …/frames` and frame details. A frame's `meta.film` already in a roll.json is read without it and dropped the next time the roll is saved. Grouping an event's frames by film stock uses the roll's.
