# FF-162 Make a site from the Browse filters

**Status:** later · **Group:** 13. Sites

## What

On the rolls page, "Make a site from these filters" (in the menu beside New roll) starts a new site whose rules are the Browse filters in force: the year as a Date shot range, and the camera, film stock and lens as the same rules (FF-119), for a quick start.
