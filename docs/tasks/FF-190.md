# FF-190 A frame's page in a site: the filmstrip above, and its details

**Status:** done · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Built in:** 13. Sites

## Why

Seeing where you are in an event before the photo, and finding out more about a photo (camera, lens, film) only when you want to.

## What

A frame's page gets the new look, with the filmstrip moved above the photo, just under the bar with "← Iceland" and "7 / 42". Under the photo, a line gives its title and date when it has a title, and the other details chosen for the site (camera, lens and film stock at first) are listed under it. _(From the [wireframes](https://claude.ai/artifact/VWNcdhqokFEmgHq6My8bqn): "Frame · filmstrip above" and "Phone · Frame". Changes FF-184's layout and caption.)_

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The bar has "← Iceland" (or "← Other 2026 photos") at the left and "7 / 42" at the right, replacing "7 of 42".
- [x] The filmstrip ("Filmstrip") is directly under the bar, centred, above the photo, the current frame outlined; it scrolls sideways on its own when it's wider than the screen. The script scrolls it so the frame shown is in its middle (as near as the strip's ends allow); its thumbnails carry their sizes, so that works before they load. _(Fixed after review: the old centring measured from the wrong box once the strip lost `position: relative`.)_
- [x] The photo is large, between thin "Previous frame" and "Next frame" arrows (none past the ends), fitted so the photo and its caption line fit on the screen.
- [x] The photo and its caption line are one figure, the line exactly as wide as the photo (not a fixed width under it), with Previous and Next level with the photo's middle: just outside it, or over its edges on a phone. _(Refined after review.)_
- [x] Under the photo, one line only for a frame with a title (and Title ticked): the title in bold and, when ticked, its date shot in grey ("Black beach · Sep 2026"). _(Changed after review: most frames have no title, so with none there's no line.)_
- [x] Under that, always shown, the other details ticked for the site, each a small grey label over its value: Date shot (when there's no title line), Camera, Lens, Film stock. A new site ticks Camera, Lens and Film stock. _(Changed after review: first built behind a small ⓘ icon with a few lines of script and a no-script stylesheet; the icon, script and stylesheet are gone.)_
- [x] On the site's page in the app, the captions choice becomes "Details to show", with the same choices; the field stays `captions` in the site file and the API, so nothing changes for saved sites.
- [x] ← and → step, Esc goes back, and a swipe steps on a phone, as now (FF-184).
- [x] On a phone the photo is full width, the filmstrip one row under the bar, and the details two to a row.

## What was built

- Domain: `renderFramePage` writes the bar ("← Frame walk", "3 / 6"), the filmstrip, then the stage: a `.frame-figure` holding the photo (with Previous and Next) and, when anything is known, its `.frame-info` caption: the title line and the `.frame-details` list. `site-script.ts` steps by key and swipe and centres the frame shown in the filmstrip. `captionOrder` is gone, and the default details are camera, lens and film stock. The design version is 6, so sites built before go out of date once.
- Stylesheet: the filmstrip under the bar (slimmer, centred when it fits, a thin line under it), the caption line and the icon, the details grid (two to a row on a phone).
- Web: the fieldset is "Details to show".
- Tests: the frame page (bar and place, filmstrip before the photo, the title line only with a title, the details always shown, the date among them with no title, the figure), the build, the web label and default, and `build-site.e2e.ts` (the camera shown under the photo).

## Not in this task

- New details (place, a description of the frame): only those the site can already show.
- Zoom, a slideshow or a download of the full size.
