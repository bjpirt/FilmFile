# FF-183 Browse a site by roll, event or both, as cards

**Status:** done · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Built in:** 13. Sites

## Why

A site that works like the app: a visitor picks a roll or an event from cards, then sees its photos.

## What

"Organise by" on a site's page (Rolls, Events or Both) replaces "Group by". A built site opens on a page of cards, one for each event or roll, like the app's: a peek of its three best photos, its name, its photo count and its date. A card opens that roll's or event's own page of photos. With Both, a "Events | Rolls" menu in the header of every page switches between the two pages of cards. _(From feedback on the first sites, FF-119 and FF-120.)_

## Acceptance criteria

The site's option:

- [x] A site's page has "Organise by" with "Rolls", "Events" and "Both", saved as other choices are; a new site starts on "Both". "Group by" and its "Nothing" are gone.

The cards:

- [x] With "Events", `index.html` is a page of event cards; with "Rolls", of roll cards; with "Both", `index.html` is the event cards and `rolls.html` the roll cards.
- [x] A card shows its three highest-rated photos on the site (the earliest first among equals; empty places if it has fewer), the event's name or the roll's number and name, "12 photos" ("1 photo") and its date (an event's own, else its first frame's), and leads to its page. The peek uses the site's smallest image until FF-184 adds a smaller one.
- [x] Cards are newest first, by the latest frame in each; frames inside a card stay in shot order.
- [x] In the Events view, frames in no event (marked "No event", FF-173, or never given one) are on a "No event set" card, last, whose page is `events/_no-event.html` (a slug never starts with `_`). _(A frame is in one event, its own or else its roll's, so "a frame in two events is on both cards" was dropped.)_
- [x] A site of one roll or one event still opens on its cards page, with one card.
- [x] With nothing on the site, the cards page says "This site has no photos yet." as now.

A roll's or event's page:

- [x] `rolls/2026-053-<the last 8 characters of the roll's ID>.html` and `events/<slug>.html`, each with a link "← All rolls" or "← All events" to its cards page, its heading ("2026-053 Westfjords"), a line under it with its date and count ("Sep 2026 · 8 photos"), and its photos as today's grid, opening in the lightbox (FF-184 replaces it with a frame page). Its title is "2026-053 Westfjords · Iceland highlights".
- [x] A roll's or event's page is made only if it has photos on the site, and a page no longer needed is gone after the next build (each build is a fresh folder swapped in, FF-120); a renumbered roll or renamed event's page moves with it.

Every page:

- [x] A header with the site's name, leading to `index.html`; with "Both", an "Events | Rolls" menu in it, the current view marked (`aria-current`), on every page, including a roll's or event's.
- [x] On a phone, cards are one column and the header and menu fit without scrolling sideways.
- [x] Every link is relative, so the site works opened from the folder and from any address (FF-120).

Building and opening:

- [x] `site.json` records the options and each photo's rating and page names, so the status says "Out of date" when "Organise by" changes, and a build that changes nothing rewrites nothing (FF-120). An older `site.json` still reads as a site's (the new fields default), so such a site is only out of date. _(Reworded: recording the pages themselves wasn't needed.)_
- [x] The service's `/built-sites/<slug>/` serves `rolls.html`, `rolls/*.html` and `events/*.html` as well as `index.html`, `assets/` and `images/`, still through the safe-path helper and nothing else (D102, widened by D104).

## What was built

- Domain (`domain/sites/`): `siteGroups` (a site's rolls or events, newest first, each with its page path, its best three photos and its date), `renderCardsPage`, `renderGroupPage` and `sitePageShell` (the header with the site's name and the "Events | Rolls" menu), `photo-shape.ts`; `renderSite` writes `index.html`, `rolls.html` with Both, a page for each event and roll, the stylesheet and the script. `render-site-page.ts` is gone.
- `GroupLabel` gained `file` (its page's name), made by `rollGroupLabel` and `eventGroupLabel`; `SitePhoto` gained `rating`. `SiteOptions` and a site's presentation have `organiseBy` (`events`, `rolls`, `both`; Both by default) in place of `groupBy`; an older site file reads as Both.
- `GetBuiltSiteFile` also serves `rolls.html`, `rolls/*.html` and `events/*.html`.
- The site's page has "Organise by" (Events, Rolls, Both) in place of "Group by".
- Tests: the domain pages, the use case and API (pages made, served and refused), an older `site.json` read, the site's page, and `build-site.e2e.ts` (the event cards, Rolls from the menu, a roll's page and the lightbox, "← All rolls").

## Not in this task

- A frame's own page with thumbnails, and captions (FF-184).
- "Both" in the preview on a site's page: FF-182's switch stays Roll | Event.
- Other orders, sections, a description on a card, or a site that skips its cards page.
- Templates (FF-186): build the pages with it in mind, as plain data in and files out.

## Notes

- Replaces `groupBy` in a site's presentation with `organiseBy`: `'rolls' | 'events' | 'both'`, default `'both'`; sites already saved aren't migrated (as FF-179 and FF-181). D104 records the layout and widens D102.
- The pages stay pure functions in `domain/sites/` (`renderSite` returns every file); the card's peek uses the site's own smallest image size.
- The best three are by rating, then shot order; the same rule as the app's `bestRated` (in the web app, so the domain needs its own).
- Needs FF-179 (presentation without `title` and `intro`) and FF-182; do after them.
- Tests: the domain's pages (cards, order, no-event card, both, file names, relative links), the build (pages removed and made, up to date), the route (new paths served, others refused), the site's page ("Organise by") and `build-site.e2e.ts` (open the site, switch to Rolls, open a roll, go back).
