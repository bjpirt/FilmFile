# FF-112 Open a roll in NegPy

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 3. Integrate with NegPy · **Was:** U65

## Why

Processing a roll's raw scans in NegPy without copying them or hunting for them in the library.

## What

"Open in NegPy" in a roll's Actions menu makes a folder for the roll in `Editors/NegPy/`, holding a link to each of its frames' Raw scans, named so NegPy fills in what it can. *(Was "Export raw scans"; a zip for other editors could come later.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] "Open in NegPy" makes `Editors/NegPy/2026-014 Nikon FE Portra 400 2026-03-14/`, named so NegPy's "Infer from Folder Name" fills in the camera, film and date.
- [ ] It holds a link to each Raw scan, named `<roll>_Frame<frame>_<original name>` (`2026-014_Frame012A_DSC04412.ARW`), and a hidden `.filmfile.json` naming the roll's ID.
- [ ] Then: "12 raw scans are ready for NegPy. In NegPy's Library, choose Refresh."
- [ ] Choosing it again adds links for raw scans added since and removes those whose version has gone; the folder is found by its `.filmfile.json` and never renamed.
- [ ] A roll with no raw scans has the item turned off, saying why.
- [ ] The first time, Settings or the message says what to do once in NegPy: Library, +, "Import Subfolders as Rolls…", and choose `Editors/NegPy`.

## Not in this task

- Bringing exports back (FF-113).

## Notes

- API: `POST /api/rolls/{rollId}/negpy`.
- Needs FF-105 (the folder) and FF-109 (read-only originals).
- Copies are made where links can't be (Windows without Developer Mode).
