# FF-192 A site's title and copyright notice

**Status:** done · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Built in:** 13. Sites

## Why

The name I give a site in FilmFile is for me (and its folder); what visitors see at the top of its pages should be my choice, or nothing at all. And every page should say whose the photographs are.

## What

A site's page gets "Site title", apart from its name, blank at first, and "Copyright notice", which starts as the archive's copyright from Settings and can be changed or cleared for that site. A built site shows the title in its header (none when blank) and the notice in a footer on every page. _(Asked for after FF-190; brings back a title apart from the name, which FF-179 had folded together.)_

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A site's page has "Site title" and "Copyright notice" above "Details to show"; each is saved when you leave it, if it changed, and leaving it unchanged saves nothing.
- [x] A new site's title is blank. With a blank title, no page names the site in its header, and the browser's tab shows the page's own name ("Iceland 2026", "Black beach"), or "Photographs" on the home page; with a title, the header shows it, linking home, and the tabs end with it ("Iceland 2026 · Iceland in September").
- [x] "Copyright notice" shows the archive's copyright from Settings until it's changed; changing it saves the site's own; clearing it saves a blank one, and the site shows no notice.
- [x] Every page of a built site ends with a footer holding the notice in small grey text over a thin line, escaped; none when it's blank.
- [x] Changing the title or the notice (or the archive's copyright, for a site that uses it) makes the site "Out of date: options changed"; renaming the site doesn't.
- [x] Sites saved before have a blank title and use the archive's copyright; an older `site.json` reads as built with no notice, so the next status says it's out of date.

## What was built

- Domain: `SitePresentation` gained `title` (blank for none) and `copyright` (null for the archive's, blank for none); `SiteOptions` has `title` and the `copyright` worked out by `SiteBuilds` (which now reads the settings). `sitePageShell` leaves out the site's name when the title is blank and writes `.site-foot` with the notice; `tabTitle` makes the tab titles.
- Contracts and API: `SitePresentation` is `{ title, copyright, captions }` (title up to 200 characters, copyright up to 500 or null), all required. The site file stores them with the rest of the presentation, only when it isn't plain; older files read with the defaults.
- Web: `presentation-fields.tsx` has "Site title" and "Copyright notice" (saved on leaving the field), the site page passes it the archive's copyright from `useSettings`.
- Tests: the domain (blank title, tab titles, the footer and none, escaping), the API (title and copyright saved and given back, the archive's copyright used, overridden and cleared, a title change out of date and a rename not, refused shapes), the site file and manifest read from older files, the web fields, and `build-site.e2e.ts` (a title and notice set, seen on the built site).

## Not in this task

- A licence, or a notice per photo: the images already carry the frame's own copyright.
- Styling the footer differently per site (FF-186's templates, later).
