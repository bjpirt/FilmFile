# FF-113 Bring in NegPy's exports

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 11. Integrate with NegPy · **Was:** U75

## Why

What I make in NegPy comes back into the catalogue with its frame, not as loose files.

## What

"Bring in NegPy exports" in a roll's Actions menu adds each file NegPy has exported into the roll's NegPy folder as a Final of its frame.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each file in the roll's NegPy folder's `export/` is added as a Final of the frame its name starts with (`2026-014_Frame012A_`), with the raw scan named after that as its parent and NegPy as its editor.
- [ ] The export is copied in and left where it is; one already brought in (the same SHA-256) is skipped, and a re-export with changes is another Final.
- [ ] Files are checked as uploads are (signature, size, pixel limit), and NegPy's unfinished `.part` files are skipped.
- [ ] Then: "3 finals added. 1 didn't match a frame: print-1.jpg; 1 isn't a JPEG, PNG, WebP or TIFF: DSC04412.jxl."

## Not in this task

- Watching the folder (FF-114).
- Choosing frames for exports that didn't match (FF-136).
