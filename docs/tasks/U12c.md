# U12c An event's frames

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 4. Metadata

## What was built

Choosing an event, on the Events page or in the browse panel's Events section, opens its page (`/events/<slug>`): the event's name, date and frame count, then one contact sheet of the frames from all its rolls, not the rolls themselves, in shot order (rolls by date shot, undated last, then roll number; frames in frame order), each labelled with its frame number and roll number. Group by Nothing, Roll, Camera, Lens or Film stock, kept in the address (`?group=roll`), each group headed by its name and frame count, a roll's heading linking to the roll, and frames with none last. Until frames have their own details (U15), a frame's camera, lens and film stock are its roll's. The rolls page's event filter is gone; Group by Event stays. The "Organise by event" journey sees an event's frames from two rolls, grouped by roll (D33).
