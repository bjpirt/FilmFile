# FF-119 Sites

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Was:** U97

## Why

I want to share a chosen set of photographs as a site, and say what goes in by describing it ("Iceland 2026, rated 4 stars and up"), not by picking frames one at a time.

## What

A Site is a saved definition: a name and a selection of frames (FF-164: rules, and frames always or never included by hand) and, with FF-120, how it's built. A "Sites" page lists them; a site's page edits its selection and shows the frames that match as they change. Always live: the frames are worked out from the archive as it is now. Building the site is FF-120.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A "Sites" link in the header, after Exports, opens a page listing the sites by name, each with its frame count ("24 frames"), and a "New site" button asking for a name.
- [ ] A site's page has its name (changeable) and its selection: the rule editor, "24 frames match" and the grid, as on Find frames (FF-164), with "Always include" and "Never include" on each frame, and a "Hidden: 2" line listing those left off with a way to put each back.
- [ ] A name makes the site's slug (`iceland-highlights`), unique among sites; creating or renaming to one that exists says so.
- [ ] Deleting a site asks "Delete site Iceland highlights?" and removes its definition; a folder it built in `Sites/` is left where it is.
- [ ] A site is saved as `Library/sites/<slug>.json` with its ID, name and `selection`, against its version token; one changed elsewhere says "This site changed since you opened it. Reload the page." and keeps what's there; writing it again with nothing changed leaves the file byte-identical.
- [ ] `GET /api/sites`, `POST /api/sites`, `GET`, `PATCH` (with `If-Match`) and `DELETE /api/sites/{slug}`; the matching frames come from `POST /api/frames/search` (FF-164) with the site's selection.

## Not in this task

- Building the site (FF-120).
- Publishing it (FF-121).
- More than one gallery, or sections, in a site.
- Adding frames to a site from the roll and event pages, or making a site from the Browse filters (FF-162, FF-163).

## Notes

- A hidden frame (FF-174, D99) is never in a site, however the selection finds it; "hidden by hand" here is a choice for one site only.
- Replaces D61 (D91). Kept in `Library/sites/`, beside `events/`, `equipment/` and `exports/` (design section 5): a site is a record like an event, so it's backed up and synced with the library.
- The rules, rule editor, count and grid are FF-164's; this task adds the record and its pages, as FF-116 does for exports.
