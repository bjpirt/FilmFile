# U7 New roll page

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 3. Rolls and the roll page

## What was built

New roll opens its own page (`/rolls/new`) instead of a field on the rolls page. It asks for the roll's details: roll number, name, description, and the date it was shot, which needs a year and can add month, day and time (D27). The roll number and year are marked required with an orange *. Scans can be dropped or chosen on the same page, and are listed until the roll is created. Create roll saves the roll with its details, uploads the scans with progress, and opens the roll's page. The details show on cards (name, and the date as "Sep 2026"), in the list (Name and Shot columns) and on the roll page. Rolls can also be sorted by roll number or date shot (D9). Starts the "Describe a roll" journey. Later layers (camera, film stock, lens, event) add their fields to this page.
