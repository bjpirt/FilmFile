# U11b Lens

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 4. Metadata

## What was built

A lens per roll, chosen from a dropdown or added with Add new… (`equipment/lenses/<slug>.json`: make and name, required, and focal length and maximum aperture; D30), kept in the roll's `meta.lens` and shown in the details row, e.g. "Nikon Nikkor AI-S 50mm f/1.8". Group by Lens, with "No lens" last, and a Lenses section in the browse panel filtering the rolls by lens (`?lens=<slug>`), as for cameras and film stocks; the roll's own lens, until frames have their own (U15).
