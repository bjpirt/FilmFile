# FF-177 Frame selections

**Status:** done · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Built in:** 13. Sites · *(Split from FF-164.)*

## Why

Sites say which frames go in by describing them ("Iceland 2026, rated 4 stars and up"), and the same description may later find frames for exports.

## What

One reusable way to pick frames: a list of rules, each with the control that suits what it's about, all of which a frame must match, plus frames always or never included by hand. This task builds the rules, how a frame is matched, the search that finds the matching frames, and the rule editor, count and grid that a page (a site's, FF-119) holds its own selection in. It has no page of its own: the "Find frames" page and exporting what's found are FF-164. Always live: the frames are worked out from the archive as it is now.

## Acceptance criteria

The rules:

- [x] **Rating:** "Rated at least" 1 to 5 stars; unrated frames don't match.
- [x] **Event:** "Event is" one or more of the archive's events, chosen from a multi-select; a frame is in its own event, else its roll's (D50).
- [x] **Roll:** "Roll is" one or more rolls, chosen from a multi-select by roll number and name.
- [x] **Camera, lens, film stock:** "Camera is", "Lens is", "Film stock is": one or more, chosen from a multi-select; a frame's camera and lens are its own, else its roll's, and its film stock is its roll's (D45).
- [x] **Date shot:** "Shot on or after" and "Shot on or before", each a year, month or day as precisely as given; a date is compared at the precision of the limit (before September 2026 includes any day in it); frames with no date, of their own or their roll's, don't match.
- [x] **Has:** "Has a Final" and "Has a Raw scan": the frame has a version of that kind.
- [x] A rule that names an event, roll, camera, lens or film stock that has since gone shows it as "no longer in the archive" and matches nothing for that value.
- [x] A frame must match every rule; with no rules every frame matches.
- [x] A hidden frame (D99) never matches, however the rules or "Always include" find it.

By hand:

- [x] A selection holds frames `pinned` ("Always include": on it whatever the rules say) and `hidden` ("Never include": never on it), by roll and frame ID; a frame can't be both; with none of either the selection is its rules alone. A pinned or hidden frame that has gone from the archive is skipped, and its entry kept.

The search:

- [x] `POST /api/frames/search` takes rules (and any pinned and hidden frames) and gives the matching frames in shot order (a frame's own date shot, else its roll's; undated last; then the roll's number and frame order), each with its roll and frame ID and why it's on it (the rules, or pinned), and their count; a rule with the wrong shape is a 422.

The pieces a page holds:

- [x] The rule editor: "Add a rule ▾" lists the kinds, each added once; each rule has the control that suits it and its own remove button.
- [x] The count ("24 frames match", "1 frame matches") and the grid of the matching frames' thumbnails, which update as rules are added, changed or removed, without saving anything; a page can put its own actions on each frame (FF-119's "Always include" and "Never include"), and the grid marks the pinned ones.

## What was built

- `domain/selections/` (rules, `matchesRule`, `whyOnSelection`, shot order), `SearchFrames` and `POST /api/frames/search` (`@filmfile/contracts/selections`).
- `apps/web/src/selections/`: `FrameSelectionEditor` (the rule editor with "Add a rule ▾", the count, "24 frames match", and the grid of thumbnails, each opening its frame), `useFrameSearch`, and `pin`, `leaveOff` and `putBack` for a page's "Always include" and "Never include".

## Not in this task

- The "Find frames" page, "Export these…", and frames chosen by hand from an export (FF-164).
- Saving a selection by itself: a site holds its own (D93; FF-119), as an export will.
- Rules joined by OR (a multi-select is the OR within a rule).
- Tags and locations as rules, until they exist (FF-132, FF-133).

## Notes

- D93. The rules are a pure function in `domain/` over a frame's resolved details (the same layers as `GetFrameDetails`, D50, D45): a rule is a discriminated union of its kind and values, tested without the filesystem. The matching frames come from the rolls repository, as the event page's do; the index (FF-122) isn't needed.
- A selection is `{ rules, pinned, hidden }`; each rule is its kind and values.
- The rule editor, count and grid are React components in `apps/web`, tested on their own; the live count asks the API, so the service is the one that decides what matches.
- Built: the rules and matching in `domain/selections/`, the `SearchFrames` use case, `POST /api/frames/search` and its contract (`@filmfile/contracts/selections`); in `apps/web/src/selections/`, `FrameSelectionEditor` (rule editor, count and grid in one, taking the page's selection, a change callback and optional per-frame actions) with `pin`, `leaveOff` and `putBack` for a page to call. A date rule being typed is kept in the editor until it is a real date, so a selection it holds is always one the service accepts; a rule with nothing chosen matches nothing, and says so.
- Tests: rule matching (each kind, ANDed, dates at different precisions, gone references, pinned and hidden), the API, and the rule editor, count and grid; no journey (FF-119's covers them).
