# FF-146 Catalogue details for darktable

**Status:** later · **Group:** Later: Integrate with darktable · **Was:** U80

## What

Before darktable first sees a raw scan, the service writes its XMP sidecar beside the link with the frame's title, rating, notes and tags (film stock, camera, lens, event), so darktable shows them and carries them into its exports. A sidecar already there is left alone, as darktable may have edited it.
