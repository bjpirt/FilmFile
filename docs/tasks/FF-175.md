# FF-175 Link events and equipment to where they lead

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 4. Metadata

## Why

Wherever a roll's event, camera, lens or film stock is named, I should be able to follow it, instead of going back and finding it myself.

## What

On a roll's page, the details row's Event leads to that event's page, and Camera, Lens and Film stock lead to the rolls page filtered to that item. On an event's page, a group heading by camera, lens or film stock does the same, and each frame's roll number leads to the roll.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A roll's details row links its Event to `/events/<slug>`, and its Camera, Lens and Film stock to `/?camera=<slug>`, `/?lens=<slug>` and `/?film=<slug>`.
- [x] An event's page, grouped by camera, lens or film stock, links each group's heading to the rolls filtered that way; the "No camera", "No lens" and "No film stock" groups aren't links.
- [x] Each frame on an event's page has its roll number as a link to the roll.

## Not in this task

- Pages for a camera, lens or film stock showing their frames (the rolls page's filter is used instead).
- Links on roll cards, the list view's columns and the frame view's details.

## What was built

`RollAbout` in `rolls/roll-page.tsx`, `FrameGroup.rollsLink` in `events/event-frames.ts` and the headings and captions in `events/event-page.tsx`, with page tests for each.
