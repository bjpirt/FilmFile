# FF-162 Make a site or export from the Browse filters

**Status:** later · **Group:** 13. Sites

## What

On the rolls page, "Make a site from these filters" and "Make an export from these filters" (in the menu beside New roll) start a new site or export whose rules are the Browse filters in force: the year as a Date shot range, and the camera, film stock and lens as the same rules (FF-164), for a quick start.

## Acceptance criteria

Written when the task is planned into a group.
