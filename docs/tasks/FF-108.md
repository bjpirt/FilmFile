# FF-108 Number rolls by date

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 2. Roll numbers · **Was:** U93

## Why

After importing a batch of rolls and giving them their dates, their numbers can follow the order they were shot in, so the negatives are filed in that order.

## What

"Number rolls…" on the rolls page renumbers every roll that isn't filed so that, in each year, their numbers follow their dates shot, after that year's filed rolls, with a preview before anything changes (D68).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] "Number rolls…" on the rolls page shows a preview: each roll not yet filed, with its number now and after ("2026-014 → 2026-009"), grouped by year and in date-shot order, then rolls shot on the same day in their current order.
- [ ] In each year, the new numbers start after the highest filed number of that year, or at 001, and are consecutive.
- [ ] A roll's year comes from its date shot, so a roll numbered in the wrong year moves to its year's numbers.
- [ ] Rolls with no date shot keep their numbers, listed apart as "No date shot: give them one to number them", and their numbers are skipped.
- [ ] Filed rolls keep their numbers and aren't listed (FF-107).
- [ ] The batch API rejects with 422 and changes nothing if asked to renumber an already-filed roll; a stale ETag still returns 409.
- [ ] When nothing would change, it says "The rolls are in order."
- [ ] Apply changes every roll in the preview together; the rolls page shows them in their new order. Each roll's folder is renamed to its new number (FF-151); swapping numbers (2026-014 ↔ 2026-015) works, as the ID keeps the names apart.
- [ ] If a roll changed since the preview (409), nothing is changed and it says to look again; Cancel changes nothing.

## Not in this task

- Patterns other than `YYYY-NNN` (FF-153).
- Numbering only some of the rolls.

## Notes

- Needs FF-151, FF-106 (the repository refuses a number another roll has) and FF-107.
- The repository gets a batch operation (the many-roll version of FF-106's `saveRenumbered`) that validates the whole final state (every number free and well formed, no roll filed or changed) and renames every folder, since a swap can no longer be done one save at a time (D70).
- API: `POST /api/rolls/numbering` with each roll's ID, version (`If-Match`) and new number; the service checks the whole change (all numbers free and well formed, no roll filed or changed) before saving any.
- Journey: import three rolls out of order without dates, give them dates, number them, see the order, file one, and see a new roll numbered after it.
