# FF-164 Frame selections

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 12. Exports

## Why

Exports and sites both need to say which frames go in by describing them ("Iceland 2026, rated 4 stars and up"), and finding frames that way is useful by itself.

## What

One reusable way to pick frames: a list of rules, each with the control that suits what it's about, all of which a frame must match. A "Find frames" page is its first use: add rules and see the frames across every roll that match, live. Exports (FF-116) and sites (FF-119) each hold their own selection and use the same rules, rule editor and grid. Always live: the frames are worked out from the archive as it is now.

## Acceptance criteria

The page:

- [ ] A "Find frames" link in the header opens a page with "Add a rule ▾", the rules added, "24 frames match", and a grid of the matching frames' thumbnails, in shot order (a frame's own date shot, else its roll's; undated last; then the roll's number and frame order), each opening the frame in its roll.
- [ ] "Export these…" on the page opens the Export dialog (FF-165) with these rules as its frames ("24 frames match", and "Edit rules" which expands the rule editor and grid inside the dialog), so what's found is exported with the same choices as any other export.
- [ ] The count and the grid update as rules are added, changed or removed, without saving anything; with no rules, every frame matches.
- [ ] Each rule is added once from "Add a rule ▾" and removed with its own button, and a frame must match all of them.

The rules:

- [ ] **Rating:** "Rated at least" 1 to 5 stars; unrated frames don't match.
- [ ] **Event:** "Event is" one or more of the archive's events, chosen from a multi-select; a frame is in its own event, else its roll's (D50).
- [ ] **Roll:** "Roll is" one or more rolls, chosen from a multi-select by roll number and name.
- [ ] **Camera, lens, film stock:** "Camera is", "Lens is", "Film stock is": one or more, chosen from a multi-select; a frame's camera and lens are its own, else its roll's, and its film stock is its roll's (D45).
- [ ] **Date shot:** "Shot on or after" and "Shot on or before", each a year, month or day as precisely as given; a date is compared at the precision of the limit (before September 2026 includes any day in it); frames with no date, of their own or their roll's, don't match.
- [ ] **Has:** "Has a Final" and "Has a Raw scan": the frame has a version of that kind.
- [ ] A rule that names an event, roll, camera, lens or film stock that has since gone shows it as "no longer in the archive" and matches nothing for that value.
- [ ] **Frames chosen by hand:** a selection can also hold specific frames, which the pages that start an export set (the frame view's frame, a roll's or event's selected frames): shown as "12 frames chosen by hand" with "Review" and a way to take any out, never added from "Add a rule ▾"; a frame must be one of them to match, besides matching any other rules.

For other pages to use:

- [ ] The rules, the rule editor, the count and the grid are one piece other pages hold in their own copy (a record's `selection`: its `rules`, as a list of kind and values, and frames `pinned` and `hidden` by roll and frame ID); a page can put its own actions on each frame in the grid (FF-119's and FF-116's "Always include" and "Never include").
- [ ] A pinned frame is on the selection whatever the rules say and a hidden one never is, a frame can't be both, and the grid marks them; with none pinned or hidden the selection is its rules alone.
- [ ] `POST /api/frames/search` takes rules (and any pinned and hidden frames) and gives the matching frames (roll and frame IDs, and why each is on it: the rules, or pinned) and their count.

## Not in this task

- Saving a selection by itself: sites and exports hold theirs (D93); saving the Export dialog's choices is FF-116.
- Rules joined by OR (a multi-select is the OR within a rule), and several galleries in one site.
- Tags and locations as rules, until they exist (FF-132, FF-133).
