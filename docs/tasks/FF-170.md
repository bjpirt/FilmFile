# FF-170 Rotate a frame from the keyboard

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 5. Describing frames

## Why

Going through a roll turning the frames that were scanned sideways, without reaching for the mouse.

## What

In the frame view, R turns the version in view a quarter turn clockwise and Shift-R anticlockwise, as the two Rotate buttons do, and each press adds a turn straight away.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] R turns the version in view clockwise and Shift-R anticlockwise, each saved with `If-Match` as the buttons' turns are, and round past upright (anticlockwise from upright is 270).
- [x] A turn shows at once, in the big image, the filmstrip and the roll page, and presses made in quick succession each add a turn, even before the first is saved.
- [x] It goes by Shift, not by the letter's case, so Caps Lock doesn't turn it the other way.
- [x] Ctrl-R, Cmd-R and Alt-R are left to the browser (reload and the like) and turn nothing; R typed in a field (a title, notes) turns nothing; R does nothing while "Delete this frame?" or "Delete this version?" is open.
- [x] A turn that's refused because the roll changed (409) or not saved is put back, with "This roll changed since you opened it. Reload the page." or "Couldn't save it. Is the service running?".
- [x] The Rotate buttons' hover text names the keys: "Rotate clockwise (R)" and "Rotate anticlockwise (Shift+R)".

## Not in this task

- Rotating from a thumbnail on the roll or event page.

## What was built

`useSaveFrame` (`frames/use-save-frame.ts`) replaces `turn(versionId, rotate)` with `turnBy(versionId, degrees)`: it works the new turn out from the version's current turn in the cached roll (already changed by earlier presses), shows it at once in the roll and the rolls list, and saves it in the queue with the frame view's other saves, putting the turn back on a refusal or a failure. `frames/frame-view.tsx` calls it for the Rotate buttons and for R and Shift-R in its key handler (which already ignores modifier keys and typing). Page tests cover the keys, repeated presses, Caps Lock, the modifier keys, a field, a 409 and a failed save, and the buttons' titles.
