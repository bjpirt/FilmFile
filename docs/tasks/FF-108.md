# FF-108 Number rolls by date

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 2. Roll numbers · **Was:** U93

## Why

After importing a batch of rolls and giving them dates, their numbers can follow the order they were shot in, before the sleeves are labelled.

## What

"Number rolls by date…" on the rolls page renumbers a year's unlocked rolls so their numbers follow their shot dates, after the year's locked rolls, with a preview before anything changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] "Number rolls by date…" on the rolls page asks which year, and shows a preview: each unlocked roll's number now and after ("2026-014 → 2026-009"), in shot-date order.
- [ ] New numbers start after the highest locked number of that year, or at 001, and skip numbers held by rolls left as they are.
- [ ] Locked rolls and rolls with no date shot are left as they are, and listed as such in the preview.
- [ ] A roll whose date shot is in another year than its number's is listed with that year's rolls.
- [ ] Rolls already in order are left alone, and "Nothing to change" shows when none would move.
- [ ] Applying it changes every roll in the preview, and the rolls list shows them in their new order; if one can't be changed, the rest stay as they were, and it says which.
- [ ] Cancel changes nothing.
