# FF-118 What an export says about itself

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 6. Exports · **Was:** U96

## Why

An export opened anywhere (Photos, Lightroom, a website) says who took it, when, on what camera and film, and where it's from.

## What

Each export carries everything the catalogue knows about its frame, written into the JPEG by sharp as it's made (D60). Raws are never written to.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An export's EXIF and XMP give its author and copyright (the archive's or the roll's), title, notes as its description, and date shot (as much of it as is known).
- [ ] They give the camera's make and model, the lens and the film stock, with the film stock and the event as keywords too.
- [ ] They give its rating, and its roll and frame number (`2026-014 / 12A`).
- [ ] Changing any of these remakes the export (FF-117).
- [ ] macOS Preview's inspector and Photos show the author, date, camera and keywords.

## Not in this task

- Location and GPS (with FF-133).
- Choosing what to leave out (FF-144).

## Notes

- sharp writes EXIF and an XMP packet into images it makes, so no exiftool.
- Tests read the metadata back from the export.
