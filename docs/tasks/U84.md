# U84 A packaged Mac app

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 10. Desktop app

## What was built

`pnpm package` builds the UI, then `FilmFile.app` and `FilmFile.dmg` in `apps/desktop/out` for Apple silicon (`apps/desktop/scripts/package.ts`, D56): rolldown bundles the app's main process and the service into `main.mjs` and `service.mjs`, beside the built UI and the two packages that can't be bundled, sharp (with its native code, kept outside the app archive) and swagger-ui-dist; `@electron/packager` makes the app, with an icon (`build/icon.svg`, made into `icon.icns` by `build/make-icon.sh`) and the bundle ID `app.filmfile.desktop`; `@electron/fuses` sets its fuses (no `ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE`, no `NODE_OPTIONS`, no inspector arguments, its archive only, checked against its hash) and signs it ad hoc again; and macOS's `hdiutil` makes the disk image, with an Applications link. Packaged, the app starts the service from the bundle, telling it where the UI is (`FILMFILE_UI`, new in the service's settings), and uses `FILMFILE_PORT` when it's given. Not signed with a Developer ID, so the first launch is with right-click › Open (FF-137). Journey (`packaged-app.e2e.ts`, skipped until the app is built): the packaged app, started from outside on a port of the journey's choosing since its fuses stop Playwright driving it, serves the UI and makes thumbnails of scans with sharp, and stops. CI builds it on macOS, runs that journey and keeps the `.dmg` for a week. About 285 MB as an app, 138 MB as a disk image.
