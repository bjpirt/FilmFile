# FF-187 Use your own site template

**Status:** later · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

Giving a site my own look, by writing or bringing a template.

## What

A site can be built with a template of your own, written as the built-in one is (FF-186): a folder with `template.json`, Liquid pages and `assets/`, given the documented data. A "Template" choice on a site's page picks it, the built-in "FilmFile" at first. _(Split from FF-186.)_

## Acceptance criteria

Written when the task is planned to be done.

## Notes

- Questions to settle: where your templates are kept (`Library/site-templates/`, so they're backed up and synced, or beside it in the FilmFile folder); how one is added (copied in by hand, or chosen in the app); a broken template's message (the file and line, and the site left as it was); checks on its files (sizes, no links out of its folder, only files a site may hold); a template written for another data version; and what the service's strict content security policy (D102) means for an author's script and styles when a site is opened in FilmFile.
- Needs FF-186.
