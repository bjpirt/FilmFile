# FF-116 Exports and their frames

**Status:** later · **Group:** 12. Exports · **Was:** U94

## Why

Choosing the photographs I want as files to share or print by describing them, once, and having that choice kept.

## What

An Export is a saved definition of a set of exported files: a name, a selection of frames (FF-177: rules, and frames always or never included by hand) and the recipe the Export dialog (FF-165, FF-167, FF-118) already has. "Save as an export…" in that dialog, from any way of exporting, saves it; an "Exports" page lists them; opening one shows the same dialog, expanded to a page with its name. Replaces marking frames for export one at a time (D97).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Export dialog has "Save as an export…" beside "Download", asking for a name and saving the frames (as chosen by hand, or as rules), the size, quality, metadata, layout and file names as they are; the export's page then opens.
- [ ] An "Exports" link in the header opens a page listing the exports by name, each with its frame count ("24 frames"), and a "New export" button asking for a name.
- [ ] An export's page is the Export dialog's form expanded: its name (changeable), the recipe's fields and its selection: the rule editor, "24 frames match" and the grid, as FF-177 gives them, with "Always include" and "Never include" on each frame, and a "Hidden: 2" line listing those left off with a way to put each back.
- [ ] A name makes the export's slug (`iceland-2048`), unique among exports; creating or renaming to one that exists says so.
- [ ] Deleting an export asks "Delete export Iceland 2048?" and removes its definition; files it made in `Exports/` are left where they are.
- [ ] An export is saved as `Library/exports/<slug>.json` with its ID, name and `selection`, against its version token; one changed elsewhere says "This export changed since you opened it. Reload the page." and keeps what's there; writing it again with nothing changed leaves the file byte-identical.
- [ ] The export dialogs (FF-165, FF-167) offer each saved export under "Start from", filling their fields with its recipe.
- [ ] `GET /api/exports`, `POST /api/exports`, `GET`, `PATCH` (with `If-Match`) and `DELETE /api/exports/{slug}`; the matching frames come from `POST /api/frames/search` (FF-177) with the export's selection.
