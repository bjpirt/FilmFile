# U6 Group by, browse panel and filters, with years

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 3. Rolls and the roll page

## What was built

Group by Nothing or Year (from the roll number), with a heading and count per group. The browse panel lists Years with counts; choosing one filters the rolls and the heading says so; All rolls clears it. The panel toggle in the header hides and shows it, and the grid spreads to fill the room. View, grouping and filter are kept in the page's address, so reload, back and forward, and bookmarks keep them (D23). Adds the "Find rolls" journey.
