# FF-115 NegPy's recipes kept

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 11. Integrate with NegPy · **Was:** U77

## Why

Every export can be made again from its raw and its recipe.

## What

When NegPy has written its edit beside the raw scan's link (`<name>.negpy`, with "Save on Export" on), it's copied into the Final's version folder as its recipe when the export is brought in (design section 9).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Final brought in from NegPy has its `.negpy` file beside it in its version folder, recorded as its recipe in roll.json.
- [ ] The frame view's versions column shows that a Final has a recipe.
- [ ] A Final with no `.negpy` beside its raw is brought in without one.
