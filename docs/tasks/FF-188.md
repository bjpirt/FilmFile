# FF-188 A site's home page: its events by year

**Status:** done · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Built in:** 13. Sites

## Why

A public site is about what happened, not which roll it was on, so a visitor should start from the events, newest first, in a look that's graphic and minimal and leaves the photos to carry the colour.

## What

A built site's `index.html` becomes its events grouped under large year headings, each event a card with one upright cover photo, its name, month and photo count. There's no Rolls view: the rolls are a short "By roll" list in small type at the foot of the page. "Organise by" goes from the site's page. The whole site takes the wireframes' look: white, black type, thin black rules, large bold headings, no colour but the photos. _(From the [wireframes](https://claude.ai/artifact/VWNcdhqokFEmgHq6My8bqn): "Home" and "Phone · Home". Replaces the cards of FF-183.)_

## Acceptance criteria

The page:

- [x] `index.html` has the site's name at the top left, linking home, and "6 events · 139 photographs" at the right (just the photographs when it has no events; "Other photos" aren't counted as events).
- [x] Under it, a section for each year that has events, newest first, headed by the year in large type over a thin black rule.
- [x] Each year holds a card for each of its events, newest first: one cover photo, cropped upright (4:5), then the event's name and "Sep · 42 photos" (its month and photo count; just the count when the month isn't known) _(the count keeps its word, so it reads on its own)_; the card links to the event's page.
- [x] An event's cover is its highest-rated frame on the site, the first in shot order when ratings tie.
- [x] An event's year and month are its date's; an event with no date takes its first frame's date shot.
- [x] Frames in no event are split by the year they were shot: each year ends with an "Other photos from 2026" card (its cover and count, no month) leading to its own page, `events/_no-event-2026.html`, with a frame page for each. Undated ones are an "Other photos" card (`events/_no-event.html`) in a last section, "Other", after the years, with any event that has no date at all. _(Changed after review, from one "No event set" card.)_
- [x] At the foot, "By roll" and a list in small grey type of the site's rolls ("2026-053 Westfjords"), newest first, each linking to its roll's page.
- [x] On a phone the cards are two to a row and the year headings smaller; nothing scrolls sideways.

The look (every page):

- [x] White, near-black text (#111) and grey (#6b6b6b, 4.5:1 or better) for secondary text, thin rules, large bold headings with tight spacing, and no colour but the photos; no shadows, rounded corners or gradients.
- [x] Pages still use no inline styles and work without a script (D102).

On the site's page in the app:

- [x] "Organise by" is gone; a site is always organised by event. A site saved with `organiseBy` still reads, and the field is dropped the next time it's saved.

Files:

- [x] `rolls.html` and the Events | Rolls menu are gone; a roll's page (`rolls/<roll page name>.html`) stays, reached from the "By roll" list, and its frame pages with it.

## What was built

- Domain (`domain/sites/`): `renderHomePage` (the counts, a section for each year with its cards, "Other", and the "By roll" footer) replaces `renderCardsPage`; `siteGroups` splits frames in no event into "Other photos from <year>" groups (`isOtherPhotos`), and `yearAndMonth` reads a date label's year and month; `sitePageShell` lost the Events | Rolls menu and gained an `aside` for the counts; `renderSite` writes `index.html`, then every event's and roll's page and their frame pages. Roll pages lead back with "← All events".
- `site-stylesheet.ts` has the new look: white, #111 text, #6b6b6b secondary text, black rules, large bold headings, system fonts, two cards to a row on a phone.
- `siteDesign` (2) is recorded in `site.json` as `design`, so a site built with the old design reads "Out of date: design changed" once; an older `site.json` reads as design 1. FF-186's template hash replaces it.
- `organiseBy` is gone from the domain, the API (`SitePresentation` is `{ captions }`; a request with it is refused) and the web; an older site file's `organiseBy` is read past and dropped on the next save. The service no longer serves `rolls.html`.
- Tests: the home page (counts, years, cards, cover, no month, no date, other photos by year and their pages, "Other", By roll, no menu or inline styles), the up-to-date check (design changed), the manifest and site file read from older files, the API, the web and `build-site.e2e.ts` (the year, an event's card, its page, a frame, a roll from the foot).

## Not in this task

- An event's page (FF-189) and a frame's page (FF-190).
- Choosing an event's cover by hand, or an event's description.
- Paging or "load more" for a site with many events.
