# FF-119 Sites and their criteria

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites · **Was:** U97

## Why

I want to share a chosen set of photographs as a site, and say what goes in by describing it ("Iceland 2026, rated 4 stars and up"), not by picking frames one at a time.

## What

A Site is a saved definition of which frames go on it: rules, each with the control that suits what it's about, all of which a frame must match, and frames added or left off by hand. A "Sites" page lists them; a site's page edits its rules and shows the frames that match as they change. Always live: the frames are worked out from the archive as it is now. Building the site is FF-120.

## Acceptance criteria

Sites and the page:

- [ ] A "Sites" link in the header, after Events, opens a page listing the sites by name, each with its frame count ("24 frames"), and a "New site" button asking for a name.
- [ ] A site's page has its name (changeable), "Add a rule ▾", its rules, the count of frames that match ("24 frames match"), and a grid of those frames' thumbnails in shot order (a frame's own date shot, else its roll's; undated last; then frame order).
- [ ] The count and the grid update as rules change, without saving first; a site with no rules matches every frame.
- [ ] Deleting a site asks "Delete site Iceland highlights?" and removes its definition; a folder it built in `Sites/` is left where it is.
- [ ] A site's name makes its slug (`iceland-highlights`), unique among sites; creating or renaming to one that exists says so.

The rules, each added once from "Add a rule ▾", removable, and all ANDed:

- [ ] **Rating:** "Rated at least" 1 to 5 stars; unrated frames don't match.
- [ ] **Event:** "Event is" one or more of the archive's events, chosen from a multi-select; a frame is in its own event, else its roll's (D50).
- [ ] **Roll:** "Roll is" one or more rolls, chosen from a multi-select by roll number and name.
- [ ] **Camera, lens, film stock:** "Camera is", "Lens is", "Film stock is": one or more, chosen from a multi-select; a frame's camera and lens are its own, else its roll's, and its film stock is its roll's (D45).
- [ ] **Date shot:** "Shot on or after" and "Shot on or before", each a year, month or day as precisely as given; a date is compared at the precision of the limit (before September 2026 includes any day in it); frames with no date, of their own or their roll's, don't match.
- [ ] **Has:** "Has a Final" and "Has a Raw scan": the frame has a version of that kind.
- [ ] A rule that names an event, roll, camera, lens or film stock that has since gone shows it as "no longer in the archive" and matches nothing for that value.

By hand:

- [ ] On a frame in the site's grid, "Always include" (pin) and "Never include" (hide) override the rules: a pinned frame is on the site whatever the rules say, a hidden one never is, and setting one clears the other.
- [ ] The grid marks pinned and hidden frames, and a "Hidden: 2" line lists those left off with a way to put each back.

Saving and failures:

- [ ] A site is saved as `Library/sites/<slug>.json`, with its changes saved against its version token; one changed elsewhere says "This site changed since you opened it. Reload the page." and keeps what's there.
- [ ] A site's record holds its ID, name, rules (as a list, each with its kind and values) and the pinned and hidden frames by roll and frame ID; writing it again with nothing changed leaves the file byte-identical.

API:

- [ ] `GET /api/sites`, `POST /api/sites`, `GET`, `PATCH` (with `If-Match`) and `DELETE /api/sites/{slug}`, and `GET /api/sites/{slug}/frames` giving the matching frames (roll and frame IDs, why each is on it: rules, pinned) for a site as saved or, with the rules in the request, as being edited.

## Not in this task

- Building the site (FF-120).
- Publishing it (FF-121).
- More than one gallery, or sections, in a site; rules joined by OR (a multi-select is the OR within a rule).
- Tags and locations as rules, until they exist (FF-132, FF-133).
- Adding frames to a site from the roll and event pages, or making a site from the Browse filters (FF-162, FF-163).

## Notes

- Replaces D61 (D91). Kept in `Library/sites/`, beside `events/` and `equipment/` (design section 5): a site is a record like an event, so it's backed up and synced with the library.
- The rules are a pure function in `domain/` over a frame's resolved details (the same layers as `GetFrameDetails`, D50, D45): a rule is a discriminated union of its kind and values; matching is tested without the filesystem.
- The matching frames are worked out from the rolls repository, as the event page's are; the index (FF-122) isn't needed.
- Tests: rule matching (each kind, ANDed, dates at different precisions, gone references), the use cases and API (with `If-Match` and the stable file), the Sites page and a site's page (rules, the live count and grid, pin and hide); no journey until FF-120's.
