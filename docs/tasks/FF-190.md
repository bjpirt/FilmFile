# FF-190 A frame's page in a site: the filmstrip above, and its details

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

Seeing where you are in an event before the photo, and finding out more about a photo (camera, lens, film) only when you want to.

## What

A frame's page gets the new look, with the filmstrip moved above the photo, just under the bar with "← Iceland" and "7 / 42". Under the photo, one line gives its title and date, and a "Details" button opens a short list of the details chosen for the site. _(From the [wireframes](https://claude.ai/artifact/VWNcdhqokFEmgHq6My8bqn): "Frame · filmstrip above" and "Phone · Frame". Changes FF-184's layout and caption.)_

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The bar has "← Iceland" (or the roll, "← 2026-053 Westfjords") at the left and "7 / 42" at the right, replacing "7 of 42".
- [ ] The filmstrip ("Filmstrip") is directly under the bar, centred, above the photo, the current frame outlined; it scrolls sideways on its own when it's wider than the screen.
- [ ] The photo is large, between thin "Previous frame" and "Next frame" arrows (none past the ends), fitted so the photo and its caption line fit on the screen.
- [ ] Under the photo, one line: the frame's title in bold and its date shot in grey ("Black beach · Sep 2026"); either is left out when unknown, and the line is gone with neither.
- [ ] "Details" opens a list under that line of the details ticked for the site, each a small grey label over its value (Camera, Lens, Film stock; the title and date shot are already on the line above), and closes it again; it starts closed, works without a script (`<details>`), and isn't shown when none of the ticked details is known.
- [ ] On the site's page in the app, the captions choice becomes "Details to show", with the same choices; sites saved with `captions` keep their choices.
- [ ] ← and → step, Esc goes back, and a swipe steps on a phone, as now (FF-184).
- [ ] On a phone the photo is full width, the filmstrip one row under the bar, and the details two to a row.

## Not in this task

- New details (place, a description of the frame): only those the site can already show.
- Zoom, a slideshow or a download of the full size.
