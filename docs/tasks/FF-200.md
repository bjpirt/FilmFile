# FF-200 Publish a site to S3

**Status:** later · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

Putting a built site on the web, in an S3 bucket, without other tools.

## What

An S3 bucket as a publish target in Settings, beside Cloudflare (FF-201), with an AWS profile from `~/.aws` and the same "Publish" and "Unpublish" on the site's page. _(Split from FF-121.)_

## Acceptance criteria

Written when the task is planned into a group.

## Notes

- Comes after FF-201, and reuses its publishing (D111): its `PublishBucket` port and signing, with AWS's endpoint and a region in place of R2's. What's sent, removed and cached is the same, and so is the site's Publish section.
- The target is kept with the site, as in FF-201; the keys come from a `~/.aws` profile, so FilmFile keeps none.
- Not in this task: setting up the bucket or CloudFront.
