# FF-117 The export folder

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 5. Exports · **Was:** U95

## Why

The photos I've chosen are always there as ordinary JPEGs, up to date, for anything else to use.

## What

The service keeps a JPEG of each frame marked for export in `Exports/`, made from the version shown, turned as it's shown, and remade or removed as frames change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A frame marked for export appears within moments as `Exports/2026/2026-014 Westfjords/2026-014-12A.jpg`, full size, from the version shown (FF-103), turned as the frame view shows it.
- [ ] A version shown that's a raw is exported from the largest preview it carries.
- [ ] Rotating the frame, choosing another version to show, or adding a version that becomes the one shown remakes its export.
- [ ] Unmarking or deleting the frame removes its export; renumbering it or changing its roll's number or name renames it.
- [ ] On starting, the service brings `Exports/` up to date: missing exports are made, changed ones remade, and ones no longer marked removed.
- [ ] Files in `Exports/` that FilmFile didn't make are left alone.
- [ ] The frame view says when a frame's export was last made.

## Not in this task

- Metadata in exports (FF-118).
- Other sizes and formats (FF-144).

## Notes

- A manifest in `Exports/` records what each export was made from, so the service knows which are out of date.
- Exports are made files, not originals: removing one is safe (D60).
