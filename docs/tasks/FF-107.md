# FF-107 Lock a roll's number

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 3. Roll numbers · **Was:** U92

## Why

Once a roll's number is written on its negative sleeve, the catalogue must never move it, or the sleeve and the catalogue disagree.

## What

"Lock roll number" in a roll's Actions menu, for when its sleeve is labelled. A locked roll's number can't be changed, by hand or by numbering by date.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] "Lock roll number" in the Actions menu locks it; a small lock shows beside the roll number on its page, and on its card and row on the rolls page, named "Roll number locked".
- [ ] On a locked roll, "Change roll number…" is turned off, saying "Unlock the roll number first".
- [ ] "Unlock roll number…" asks first: "Only unlock it if its sleeve isn't labelled yet."
- [ ] The API refuses to change a locked roll's number.

## Not in this task

- Numbering rolls by date (FF-108).

## Notes

- roll.json: `numberLocked: true`, only when set.
- API: `PATCH /api/rolls/{rollId}` takes `numberLocked`.
