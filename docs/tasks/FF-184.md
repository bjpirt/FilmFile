# FF-184 A frame's own page in a site, with thumbnails below

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 13. Sites

## Why

Looking at one photo large and moving through the others, as the app's frame view does.

## What

Clicking a photo on a roll's or event's page (FF-183) opens that frame's own page: a bar with "← 2026-053 Westfjords" and "12 of 36", the photo large with ‹ and › beside it, the chosen caption under it, and a filmstrip of the roll's or event's frames along the bottom. It replaces the lightbox. A frame has a page for each roll or event page it's on, so its filmstrip and stepping follow where you came from. _(From feedback on the first sites, FF-119 and FF-120; took over FF-178's captions.)_

## Acceptance criteria

The page:

- [ ] Clicking a photo on a roll's or event's page opens its frame page; the lightbox, its script and its styles are gone.
- [ ] A frame page has the site's header (FF-183), then a bar with a link back to its roll or event ("← 2026-053 Westfjords", "← Iceland 2026") and its place ("12 of 36"), then the photo, fitted to the screen, from the site's image sizes with `srcset`.
- [ ] "Previous frame" (‹) and "Next frame" (›) links step through the roll's or event's frames in shot order; Previous is hidden on the first frame and Next on the last.
- [ ] A filmstrip ("Filmstrip") along the bottom has a thumbnail link for each of the roll's or event's frames on the site, the current one marked (`aria-current`) and scrolled into view.
- [ ] Without a script every link works; with it, ← and → step, Esc goes back to the roll's or event's page, and a swipe left or right steps on a phone.
- [ ] On a phone the photo is full width with small ‹ › over its edges, and the filmstrip is one row that scrolls sideways; nothing scrolls the page sideways.

Captions (from FF-178):

- [ ] The caption is one muted line under the photo, above the filmstrip: the ticked details in the order title, date shot, camera, lens, film stock, joined with " · "; a detail the frame doesn't have is left out, and with none known (or none ticked) there's no line.
- [ ] Captions are only on frame pages: the roll and event pages' grids have none (their `<figcaption>` and its styling go).
- [ ] Changing a caption choice makes the site "Out of date", and building again changes the frame pages.

Files:

- [ ] A frame's pages are `rolls/<roll page name>/<frame number>-<last 8 of its ID>.html` and `events/<slug>/<frame number>-<last 8>.html` (`frame-<last 8>.html` for a frame with no number; odd numbers such as `12A` and `14-15` kept); a renumbered frame's pages move on the next build and old ones are removed.
- [ ] Each frame has a new 240-pixel image for the filmstrip (and the cards' peek, FF-183), made, named by hash and kept up to date as the other sizes are.
- [ ] The service's `/built-sites/<slug>/` also serves the frame pages under `rolls/*/` and `events/*/`, through the safe-path helper and nothing else (widens D104).
- [ ] Every link is relative, so the site still works from its folder and from any address.

## Not in this task

- A "More info" panel of a frame's details: dropped (FF-185), since the caption shows the details chosen.
- Wrapping round at the ends, a slideshow, zoom, or a download of the full size.

## Notes

- Needs FF-183 (roll and event pages); do after it. D105 records the frame pages; it widens D104's served paths.
- The pages stay pure functions in `domain/sites/`; the script stays plain and small (keys, swipe, scrolling the filmstrip).
- The image sizes become 240, 480, 960 and 1600 (`site-image-sizes.ts`); the 240 is not in a photo's `srcset` on the grid unless it helps.
- Tests: the domain's frame pages (bar, place, stepping and its ends, filmstrip, captions and missing details, file names and odd numbers), the build (pages moved and removed, up to date, a caption change makes it out of date), the route (new paths served, others refused) and `build-site.e2e.ts` (open a roll, a photo, step with →, go back with Esc; a phone-sized step: swipe).
