# FF-149 Rate from a thumbnail

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 5. Describing frames

## Why

Rating a roll's frames straight from its contact sheet, without opening each.

## What

Pointing at a thumbnail shows all five of its stars (FF-148), the rest outlined, and clicking one rates the frame rather than opening it, on the roll page, the event page and in the filmstrip (D62). *(Split from FF-100.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pointing at a thumbnail shows all five stars, those beyond its rating outlined (★★★★☆), and an unrated frame shows five outlined stars (☆☆☆☆☆).
- [ ] Pointing at a star fills it and the stars before it in a dimmer amber, as a preview of the rating a click would give, with a pointer cursor, so it's clear a click rates the frame rather than opening it.
- [ ] Each star is named "Rate 3 stars" for screen readers and on hover, and the current rating's star "Clear rating"; an unrated frame's stars are named "Not rated" as a group.
- [ ] Clicking a star rates the frame with that many stars, without opening it, on the roll page, the event page and in the filmstrip, saved with `If-Match`.
- [ ] Clicking the star of the rating it already has clears it, as in the frame view (D39).
- [ ] The new rating shows at once on that thumbnail, and straight away wherever else that frame is showing (the filmstrip and the frame view's heading).
- [ ] A save refused because the roll changed (409) puts the stars back as they were and says so, as rating in the frame view does.
- [ ] On touch screens, with no pointing, thumbnails show the filled stars only and a tap opens the frame as now; rating is done in the frame view.
- [ ] The thumbnail stars aren't in the Tab order: from the keyboard, frames are rated in the frame view (its stars, or the 1 to 5 keys), as now.
- [ ] While renumbering, the stars show but can't be clicked; while bulk editing, they're hidden (FF-148).

## Not in this task

- Rating several frames at once (from bulk edit's Set menu).
- Rating from the keyboard or by touch on thumbnails.

## Notes

- Needs FF-148.
- The stars sit outside the thumbnail's link, or stop the click reaching it, so rating never opens the frame.
- Saving uses the frame view's queued saves (`useSaveFrame`) in the filmstrip, and the roll's save with `If-Match` on the roll and event pages.
- Tests: roll page, event page and frame view tests for pointing, the preview, rating and clearing by click, a 409, touch (no pointing) and renumbering. No journey.
