# FF-106 Change a roll's number

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 2. Roll numbers · **Was:** U25

## Why

Before its negatives are filed, a roll's number is only provisional: a wrong guess at the year, or a roll that should come before another, is put right by hand.

## What

A roll that isn't filed has its number changed in Edit details, like its name and date; its folder is renamed to match (`2026-009-Q2P7RM4K`, FF-151). A filed roll's number can't be changed (FF-107). *(From 1.3.15, 1.4.3 and 1c.2.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Edit details on a roll that isn't filed has a Roll number field, with the current number.
- [ ] A number that isn't `YYYY-NNN`, or is another roll's, is refused, saying which, and nothing is saved.
- [ ] Saving changes the number on the roll page, in its address, on the rolls page and on its frames' pages, and renames the roll's folder to `<new number>-<ID>` (FF-151).
- [ ] Links to the roll and its frames keep working, as they use its ID.
- [ ] A change refused because the roll changed (409) says so and changes nothing.
- [ ] On a filed roll, Edit details shows the number but not as a field, with "Filed: unmark it to change the number."

## Not in this task

- Numbering many rolls at once (FF-108).
- Marking a roll as filed (FF-107).

## Notes

- Needs FF-151 (the folder naming and renaming).
- API: `PATCH /api/rolls/{rollId}` takes `roll`, refused (409) when the roll is filed or the number is taken. The repository's old `rename` (to a folder named by the number alone) is replaced by FF-151's.
