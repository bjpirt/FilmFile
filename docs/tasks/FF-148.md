# FF-148 Stars on thumbnails

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 1. Multi-version frames, finished off · **Was:** U31

## Why

So the frames worth a look stand out without opening each, and a frame says the same about itself wherever it's shown.

## What

Every frame thumbnail shows the frame's rating as small filled stars at its top left (D62). The marks U69 and U70 added to the roll page (its own details or notes, and more than one version) come to the event page and the filmstrip, so the roll page, the event page and the frame view's filmstrip show the same marks. *(Split from FF-100.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a roll's page, an event's page and the frame view's filmstrip, a frame rated 1 to 5 shows that many filled stars at its thumbnail's top left (★★★★ for 4), and a frame with no rating shows none.
- [ ] The stars sit straight on the picture, in the accent colour, with a soft shadow so they read over a bright scan; no badge behind them. In the filmstrip they're smaller, in the same corner.
- [ ] The stars are named "Rated 4 of 5" for screen readers and on hover.
- [ ] In the filmstrip, whose thumbnails are named by their links, the link's name says the rating ("Frame 12, rated 4 of 5").
- [ ] Rating a frame in the frame view updates its stars in the filmstrip straight away, and on the roll and event pages when they're next shown.
- [ ] While bulk editing on the roll page, the stars are hidden and the checkbox takes the corner; they come back on Done. While renumbering, they show as usual.
- [ ] The event page and the filmstrip also show U69's icon (details of its own or notes) and U70's (more than one version) at the bottom left, named as on the roll page.

## Not in this task

- Rating by clicking a thumbnail's stars (FF-149).
- Showing U69 and U70's icons anywhere else (rolls page strips, events page cards).

## Notes

- Ratings are one to five stars, or none (D39).
- The stars and icons move into one shared piece drawn with `Thumbnail` (from the roll page's `FrameMarks`), so the three places can't drift apart.
- The frame view's heading keeps its filled stars (★★★★) beside the frame number.
- Tests: roll page, event page and frame view tests for rated and unrated frames, bulk editing hiding the stars, and the icons on the event page and filmstrip. No journey.
