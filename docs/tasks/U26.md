# U26 Open a frame

**Status:** done · **Built in:** 5. Describing frames

## What was built

Clicking a frame's thumbnail, on a roll's page or an event's page, opens it large, filling the screen, with its frame and roll numbers. Along the bottom, a filmstrip of thumbnails of every frame in the collection it was opened from (the roll in frame order, or the event's frames in their grouping and order), the current one highlighted and kept in view; clicking one goes straight to it. Previous and next arrows on screen, and the ← and → keys, step through the same collection; Esc, or the link back, returns to where it was opened from. Each frame has its own address (`/rolls/<roll>/frames/<frame>`, or `/events/<event>/frames/<frame>` with the event's grouping), so reload and back keep it. Stepping replaces the address, so Back leaves the frame view; the filmstrip's thumbnails are named by their links. Extends the "Catalogue a roll" journey. *(From 1b.8.)*
