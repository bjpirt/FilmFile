# FF-119 Choose an event's frames for its site

**Status:** to do · **Group:** 7. Event sites · **Was:** U97

## Why

A site shows the best of an event, not every frame shot at it.

## What

On an event's page, the frames on its site are those rated at least a chosen number of stars, plus any added and less any left off by hand.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An event's page has "On the site: rated 4 stars and up" (All, or 1 to 5 stars), saved with the event.
- [ ] Frames on the site are marked on the event's page, and the page says how many: "24 frames on the site".
- [ ] Selecting frames on the event's page offers "Add to site" and "Leave off site", which override the rating for those frames.
- [ ] Changing a frame's rating moves it on or off the site, unless it was added or left off by hand.

## Not in this task

- Making the site (FF-120).

## Notes

- Kept in the event's file: `site: { minRating, add, leaveOff }`, with frames by ID.
