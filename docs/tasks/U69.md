# U69 Own details or notes as an icon

**Status:** done · **Built in:** Multi-version frames

## What was built

On a roll's page, the "Own" mark on a frame's thumbnail is now a small "lines of text" icon (`text-icon.tsx`) at its bottom left, the size of the delete icon, shown when the frame has a camera, lens, events or date shot of its own that differ from its roll's, or notes (D49). Its words are hidden text and its hover title: "Its own camera and lens", "Has notes" or "Its own lens, and notes" (`own-details.ts`, which now counts a frame's own date, U59). Nothing for a frame with neither.
